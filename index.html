GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ChillPill Gaming Cafe in Budhanilkantha, Kathmandu.

Where is ChillPill Gaming Cafe located?
We are located in Budhanilkantha, Kathmandu, Nepal — easily accessible from Lazimpat, Maharajgunj, Gongabu, and Shivapuri areas. Use the Google Maps link on this page to get exact directions to our cafe.
How much does it cost? What are the hourly rates?
PS5 cabin: NPR 300/hour · PS4 cabin: NPR 250/hour. The rate covers the whole cabin — so two friends can split it and game together. An extra joystick for 2-player is only NPR 50 more.
Can I book a cabin in advance?
Yes! Message us on WhatsApp at +977 9765130636 with your preferred date, time, and cabin type (PS5 or PS4). We will confirm your reservation right away.
Do you serve food and drinks?
Yes — we have a full cafe menu! Hot and cold coffees, specialty drinks, cold beverages, pastries, and hot snacks. You can order directly from your gaming cabin without leaving your seat.
What games are available to play?
Our game library includes FIFA / EA FC, Call of Duty, GTA V, Mortal Kombat, Spider-Man, racing games, and many more on both PS5 and PS4. We take custom game requests too — just ask our staff!
Is ChillPill good for group hangouts or birthdays?
Absolutely! ChillPill is Kathmandu's best gaming hangout spot for groups and friends. Book multiple cabins for your squad. We also accommodate birthday celebrations — ask us about group packages.
What are your opening hours?
We are open every day from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM — including weekends and public holidays. No appointment needed for walk-ins, though we recommend booking ahead on busy days.
